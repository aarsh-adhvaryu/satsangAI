Before we begin

So I can be of real help, I remember a few durable things about you between conversations — where you live, what you are working through, how you like to be spoken to. You can see, edit or delete any of it at any time under memory, and you can pause it entirely.

Some things I never keep: anything about self-harm, abuse, trauma, your health, or legal trouble. Those stay in the moment and are gone when it ends.

Separately — may I keep our conversations to help improve this companion? You can say no and everything still works exactly the same.
